May Allah put barakah in our relationship and our future, strengthen the love between us, and protect us from anything that could destroy our peace.

I pray that if we are truly meant for each other, Allah makes our journey to marriage easy, beautiful and blessed. May He make us good partners for one another, people who bring each other closer to Him, and may He grant us a future filled with peace, happiness, prosperity, good health and beautiful memories.

I pray that one day we will look back at this stage of our lives and smile because we made it through everything together. I pray that we will build a beautiful home filled with love, understanding, laughter and peace. And baby, I want you to know something very clearly: I am not loving you today simply because it is our anniversary. I love you every day. Today just gives me another reason to remind you.
